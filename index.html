Malaysia Department of Environment (DOE) APIMS / IPU Classification Standards
0 - 50 (Baik / Good)
Low pollution without any bad effect on health. Safe for all outdoor activities.
51 - 100 (Sederhana / Moderate)
Moderate pollution that does not pose health hazards to the general public.
101 - 200 (Tidak Sihat / Unhealthy)
Worsening health condition of high-risk people (heart & lung complications). General public experience irritation.
201 - 300 (Sangat Tidak Sihat / Very Unhealthy)
Worsens health and causes symptoms in the general population. Vigorous outdoor exertion should be avoided.
> 300 (Berbahaya / Hazardous)
Hazardous to highly sensitive people and general public. Stay indoors and seal ventilation.
