Why Runners Need AQI Precision

During running, minute ventilation escalates from ~6 L/min to 80-140 L/min. Mouth breathing bypasses the nasal mucociliary filter, allowing fine PM2.5 to penetrate straight into alveolar tissues.

RECOMMENDED AIR PROTOCOL
01
AQI 0 - 50 (Good)

No restriction. Ideal for 90+ min weekend long runs, interval ladders, and tempo blocks.

02
AQI 51 - 100 (Moderate)

Safe for general runners. Cap high-intensity sprints under 30 mins. Avoid highway roadsides.

03

Move speed sessions to treadmill. Limit outdoor easy jogs to 30-40 mins Max in green parks.

04

Outdoor running prohibited. Use an air-conditioned indoor treadmill with HEPA air purifiers.
