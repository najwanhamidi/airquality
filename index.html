🏃 Sports Science & Minute Ventilation

At rest, a runner breathes ~6-8 L/min. During running, minute ventilation surges to 80-140 L/min. Because mouth breathing bypasses natural nasal filtration, fine PM2.5 deposits deep in alveolar capillaries.

RUNNER PROTOCOL MATRIX
AQI 0 - 50 (Good)

Safe for all intensities. Perfect for 90+ min weekend long runs, intervals, and hill sprints.

AQI 51 - 100 (Moderate)

Generally safe. Cap track intervals under 30 mins. Choose shaded tree parks away from heavy expressways.

AQI 101 - 150 (Sensitive Groups)

Move speedwork to treadmill. Easy jogs limited to 30-40 mins Max in green reserves. Saline nasal rinse post-run.

AQI > 150 (Unhealthy)

Outdoor running prohibited. Move exclusively to an indoor air-conditioned treadmill with HEPA air filtration.
